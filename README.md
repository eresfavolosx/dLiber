> **Historical Venture Archive:** dLiber was an early student logistics and campus commerce startup founded to dramatically lower the cost of college textbooks and essential academic supplies for university students across Puerto Rico through scheduled campus delivery routes and peer-to-peer exchange.

---

## 💡 Venture Vision & Background

College textbooks and academic materials impose a heavy financial burden on undergraduate students. dLiber addressed this by:
1. Sourcing affordable textbooks and course materials directly.
2. Operating a scheduled weekly round-trip delivery route across multiple university campuses in Puerto Rico.
3. Providing an online catalog where students could buy, sell, or request supplies ahead of each campus drop-off.

---

## 🛠️ Architecture & Tech Stack

- **Design System:** Google Material Design Lite (MDL) for a clean, mobile-first card interface.
- **Interactions:** jQuery and `parallax.min.js` delivering fluid hero transitions and responsive category browsing.
- **Responsive Layout:** Optimized for mobile browser browsing on iOS Safari and Android Chrome.
